feat: guard tokenizers and reclaim safe chunk capacity

Add Backend::max_of and Backend::max_of_with_overhead (Rust) and the guard_encodings and
guard_tokenizer_files arguments of TextChipper (Python). The combined counter reports the largest
count over the primary tokenizer and every guard, so strict mode guarantees max_length for each of
them. The maximum of monotone counters stays monotone, joins are additive only when every member's
are, and the window search is seeded with the smallest bytes-per-token ratio.

When adjacent text blocks do not fit together, reclaim unused chunk capacity from the next block
at a sentence or line boundary. Recount the delivered text before accepting it; code blocks stay
intact. On the pinned 12-workload guard benchmark this raised natural-workload mean fill from 80.3%
to 87.1%, with zero overflow across seven tokenizers.

The strict guarantee remains relative to the configured tokenizers; the README documents this
limit and the cross-tokenizer benchmark. The crate version is not bumped here.
